## Family Time Guard – Phase 1

**Download `FamilyTimeGuard.zip`, extract it, and follow `INSTALL-GUIDE.txt`.**

What it does:
- Bedtime lock: warnings 15/5/1 minutes before, then the computer logs off. Signing in again at night logs off again.
- Daily game time (Roblox, Minecraft, Steam/Epic/Xbox games, web game sites). Games close when time is up.
- Daily YouTube time. The YouTube tab closes and YouTube is blocked when time is up.
- Separate school-day and weekend rules, plus holiday dates.
- Tray icon showing time left; pop-up warnings at 10 and 2 minutes.
- Parent options with a PIN: extra time, pause rules, resume.
- Activity log of games, YouTube videos, bedtime log-offs and parent actions.
