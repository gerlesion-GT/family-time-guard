FAMILY TIME GUARD - PHONE REMOTE (TELEGRAM)
===========================================

Control the kids' computers from anywhere with Telegram on your phone.
Only you can use it: each computer is paired with your Telegram account and
ignores messages from anyone else.


SETTING IT UP (about 3 minutes per computer)
--------------------------------------------
Each computer needs its OWN bot: one for Boong's PC, one for Chip's PC.

On the child's computer, in YOUR parent account:
  1. Start > Family Time Guard > "Set up phone remote (parents)"
     (the installer also offers this). Click "Yes" when Windows asks.
  2. Follow the steps in the window:
       - Open https://web.telegram.org in the browser and sign in.
       - Talk to @BotFather: send /newbot, choose a name (e.g. "Chip PC Guard")
         and a username ending in "bot" (e.g. chip_pc_guard_bot).
       - Copy the key BotFather gives you and paste it into the setup window.
         Keep this key private.
  3. The window shows a 6-digit code. Open your new bot in Telegram (on your phone
     or in Telegram Web), tap Start and send:   /pair 123456   (your code)
  4. You get "Paired!" in Telegram. Sign out of Telegram Web on the child's computer.

Tip: pin both bot chats in Telegram so they are easy to find.


COMMANDS
--------
  /status               time left right now, and bedtime
  /today                what was played and watched today
  /rules                current daily limits and bedtimes

  /bonus games 30       30 extra minutes of games today
  /bonus youtube 15     15 extra minutes of YouTube today

  /set school games 60      change the daily limit (school days)
  /set weekend youtube 90   change the daily limit (weekends)
                            use -1 for "no limit", 0 for "not allowed"
  /bedtime school 21:30     change bedtime on school nights (Sun-Thu)
  /bedtime weekend 22:30    change bedtime on weekend nights (Fri-Sat)
  /wakeup school 06:30      change the morning unlock time

  /pause 2h             switch all rules off for a while (also bedtime)
  /resume               rules on again
  /lock                 lock the computer now, until tomorrow morning
  /lock 1h              lock it for an hour
  /unlock               end the lock

  /help                 show the commands

If one computer is used by both kids, add the name: /bonus Chip games 30


WHAT YOU RECEIVE AUTOMATICALLY
------------------------------
  - "Ask for more time" requests from the kids, with buttons to approve or say no.
    (Kids can ask up to 5 times a day; requests expire after 30 minutes.)
  - Alerts: game or YouTube time used up, logged off at bedtime, signing in during
    the night, the clock helper being stopped, wrong parent PIN attempts, and when
    someone uses the parent options on the computer.
  - An evening summary at 21:30: minutes used, games played, YouTube videos watched.
    (Change the time with "dailySummaryAt" in the settings, or leave it empty for none.)


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
  - Messages reach a computer only while it is switched on and online. Commands sent
    while it is off are handled as soon as it comes back on.
  - The computer only connects out to Telegram; nothing on it is opened to the internet.
  - If Telegram only works with a VPN on the kids' home network, the computers cannot
    reach it either. A proxy can be set with "proxy" in the telegram settings.
  - To disconnect: run "Set up phone remote" again with a new bot, or uninstall.
    To stop a bot completely, send /deletebot to @BotFather.
